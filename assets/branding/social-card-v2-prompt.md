Generada con la herramienta integrada ImageGen el 30 de septiembre de 2026. Maestro: `social-card-master-v2.png`. Exportación web: `public/social-card-v2.jpg`, 1200 × 630 px, JPEG 88, mediante `npm run assets:brand`.

## Prompt final

Create a finished premium photographic social sharing card for the existing Spanish public-services website espana.chat. Deliver a wide landscape image, exactly 1200 by 630 pixels if possible, aspect ratio 1.905:1. Full-bleed beautiful editorial photograph of the Plaza de España in Seville at warm late-afternoon golden hour, elegant Spanish Renaissance architecture and ceramic details, a charming arched bridge and canal reflection centered, warm terracotta, golden sunlight, deep blue water and a little greenery. Realistic beautiful cinematic photography, understated and contemporary, inviting, vivid but natural, no illustration, no collage, no postcard clichés. Include a few small distant everyday visitors for scale, no close-up faces. Design for WhatsApp cropping the image to a tiny square thumbnail: all important imagery and branding MUST be within the central square from x=285 to x=915; the iconic central arch/bridge and Spanish architectural character must remain recognizable in that crop. Exact and ONLY visible text: 'España.' with correct ñ, a large elegant high-contrast white editorial serif wordmark reminiscent of Rhymes Display, centered horizontally, spanning no more than 530 pixels, in the lower middle of the CENTRAL SAFE SQUARE, around baseline y=420. The final period is a small warm coral-red dot. Use a subtle local dark navy photographic shadow/gradient behind the wordmark so it is exceptionally legible at 90 by 90 pixels, without darkening the beautiful sunny architecture in the upper half. No tagline, no URLs, no tiny text, no logos in corners, no borders, no UI controls, no official government seal. The photo should be the main attraction and the single centered wordmark should look tasteful and legible in both the full wide card and a square center crop. Produce a polished final raster card, not a mockup or a screenshot of a card.
